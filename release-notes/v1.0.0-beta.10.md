## What's new

- **Support** - a new page at the bottom of the menu's sidebar, above Quit: a few words from me and a "Buy me a coffee" button (GitHub Sponsors, one time). Completely optional - VBoy Color stays free.
- **Yuno's Adventure** - on the same page, a little endless runner in the Virtual Boy's reds, starring my cat Yuno. Jump over rocks, bushes and gaps with A (any face button on a gamepad), Space or Enter, a click, a tap, or the trigger pointed at it. Your best score is kept. Leaving the game, the page or the menu ends the run.

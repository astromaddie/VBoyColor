## What's new

- **Support** - a new page at the bottom of the menu's sidebar, above Quit: a few words from me and a "Gift me a coffee" button (GitHub Sponsors, one time). Completely optional - VBoy Color stays free.
- **Yuno's Adventure** - on the same page, a little endless runner in the Virtual Boy's reds, starring my cat Yuno. Jump over rocks, bushes and gaps with A (any face button on a gamepad), Space or Enter, a click, a tap, or the trigger pointed at it. Your best score is kept. Leaving the game, the page or the menu ends the run.
- **No console window on Windows** - VBoy Color (and VBoyColorVR) open just their own window now. The log is still there for bug reports: Settings > Troubleshooting > Log window shows it. If the app can't start (no Vulkan driver, no VR runtime), a message box says why.
- **Windows: the menu's logo** - the sidebar now shows the VBoy Color logo instead of plain "VBoy Color" text.

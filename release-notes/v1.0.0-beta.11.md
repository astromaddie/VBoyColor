## What's new

- **Touch controls off** (Android) - Settings > Controls > Touch controls: switched off, the on-screen controls stay out of the way - the game keeps the whole screen, and they show (over it) when you touch it, going again a few seconds after your last touch. Arranging them is now Settings > Arrange touch controls.
- **Back opens the menu** (Android) - a quick back gesture or tap during a game now always opens the menu (it was sometimes missed).
- **Smoother games on phones** (Android) - games no longer stutter on phones with a modest GPU:
  - the **LED look** now takes a fraction of the graphics work, with the same picture (lighter on the Quest and PC too);
  - a game's **save is written in the background** - games that save often (like Wario Land) no longer freeze for a moment every few seconds;
  - **steadier frame pacing**, a higher priority for the emulation, and (Android 11 and newer) the app asks the phone for a refresh rate that suits a 50 fps game.
- **Settings > Troubleshooting** (the Log window on Windows) is now only in development builds.

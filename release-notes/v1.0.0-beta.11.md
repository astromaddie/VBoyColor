## What's new

- **Touch controls off** (Android) - Settings > Controls > Touch controls: switched off, the on-screen controls stay out of the way - the game keeps the whole screen, and they show (over it) when you touch it, going again a few seconds after your last touch. Arranging them is now Settings > Arrange touch controls.
- **Back opens the menu** (Android) - a quick back gesture or tap during a game now always opens the menu (it was sometimes missed).
- **Performance overlay** - Settings > Troubleshooting > Performance overlay shows, over the game, the screen's frame rate and frame times (and frames that came late), the game's frames shown and emulated, how long emulating and coloring a frame take, and catch-ups and dropped frames - handy if a game ever feels choppy. On Android it's also in the log (logcat tag VBoyColor, "[Perf]").

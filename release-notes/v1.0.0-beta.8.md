## What's new

- **Wario Land** - the minigames and the hub, painted part by part:
  - Wario in the minigames: his face in the coin game's front poses, the hat in his form's color when he's seen from behind on the BET screen, and the far-away Wario in the heart game (and in the levels' far plane) with his hat, face, overalls and gloves instead of plain orange.
  - Wario's laugh in the elevator: a yellow shirt and white gloves rubbing together, instead of an orange shirt.
  - The hub's host: purple all over (his belly's plain patches too), with white eyes and fang, on a wooden stool - instead of a red head and patches of orange, brown and grey.
  - The sleeping creature on the save point's map: the host's purple, with a light blue snot bubble.
  - The minigame bats: green all over, with no red patches.
  - The heart game's flying creatures: light blue with a yellow eye, instead of an orange outline.
- **Close game and Quit** - at the bottom of the menu's sidebar (press twice): Close game goes back to the library with nothing running (its save written); Quit closes VBoy Color - on PC, and on Android phones and TVs (with a gamepad, there's no other way out). Not on the Quest, whose own menu closes apps.
- **Screenshots** - Save states > Screenshot (F12 on PC) saves the game's picture as a PNG, in its colors and three times its size, into a new folder of its own: the games folder's `screenshots` by default, or one picked in Settings > Folders > Screenshots.
- **Hotkeys** (PC) - Settings > Controls > Hotkeys: any key, alone or with Ctrl / Alt / Shift (Alt+F1...), for the menu, fullscreen, screenshot, save / load state, previous / next slot, reset, close game and quit. Defaults: Tab, Alt+Enter, F12, F2, F4, F6, F7, Ctrl+R, none, Ctrl+Q.
  - The color pack and video tools (F4 to F12: captures, re-import, recording...) are now off by default - turn them on in the same page; while on, those keys are theirs.
- **Updates** - when the check at start finds a newer version, a notice says so over the game or the menu, like the achievement pop-ups. The Windows app no longer downloads the RetroArch core's zip by mistake ("Not a VBoy Color package").

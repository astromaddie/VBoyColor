## What's new

- **Wario Land** - the minigames and the hub, painted part by part:
  - Wario in the minigames: his face in the coin game's front poses, the hat in his form's color when he's seen from behind on the BET screen, and the far-away Wario in the heart game (and in the levels' far plane) with his hat, face, overalls and gloves instead of plain orange.
  - Wario's laugh in the elevator: a yellow shirt and white gloves rubbing together, instead of an orange shirt.
  - The hub's host: purple fur and a brown muzzle, instead of a red head.
  - The minigame bats: green all over, with no red patches.
  - The heart game's flying creatures: light blue with a yellow eye, instead of an orange outline.
- **Updates** - when the check at start finds a newer version, a notice says so over the game or the menu, like the achievement pop-ups. The Windows app no longer downloads the RetroArch core's zip by mistake ("Not a VBoy Color package").

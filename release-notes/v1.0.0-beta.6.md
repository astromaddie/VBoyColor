## What's new

- **Android phones, tablets and handhelds** - the same APK now plays on them, with touch controls you can move and resize, or a gamepad.
- **Gamepads fixed everywhere** - PC window, PC VR and Android: the usual layout (A right, B bottom), every connected pad, pads plugged in later.
- **Settings per game** - hold a game's card in the library to give it its own button mapping (and, on a phone, its own touch controls).
- **3D on a normal screen** - side by side, top and bottom, or colored glasses (red/cyan, green/magenta, amber/blue).
- **Choose your folders** - games, saved games, save states and settings wherever you want them.
- **Updates from the app** - About → Update.
- **Dragon Hopper** - a first color pack.
- **Hand fixes to the colors** - Wario's face on the Wario Land title, Jack Frost's face in Jack Bros., Panic Bomber's moon.
